# Paired wheel-layout coverage comparison

**DRAFT / isolated ACTS vacuum PROTOTYPE.** Paired end-to-end vacuum chamber/field comparison with identical primary IDs, directions, launch, world and numerical settings. Both chamber layout and ECT field change; chamber-only attribution is not established. Unresolved-event bounds are not confidence intervals.

All momenta are total local entrance momentum. The endcap region is the fixed initial-direction |eta| cut; precision cap banks are not added to the full-sphere denominator. The cap-pair estimate combines independent positive and negative strata with their solid-angle weights.

Table entries are **proven–possible availability [%]** and paired **change bounds [percentage points]**. They include all unfinished/native-navigation-uncertain events. These intervals are unresolved-event bounds, not statistical confidence intervals. Separate endpoint sampling errors, definitely gained/lost counts, positive/negative ends, per-wheel results and status contingencies are retained in JSON/CSV.
