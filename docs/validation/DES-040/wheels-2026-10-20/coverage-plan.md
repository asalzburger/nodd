Native low-momentum gap attribution additionally needs native wheel-plane roots even where no chamber is present, or an explicit zero-gap geometry control; do not invent roots from trajectory polylines. Aligned wheel gaps are correlated and their single-wheel fractions must not be multiplied as independent efficiencies.

Retain the established analytic zero/constant-field, finite-bounds, curved-edge, coplanar-owner and tangent/paired-counter controls. Add new-wheel corner/border/gap rays, enlarged-world exit tests, field-extension seam/old-domain parity, and common-particle indexed versus bounded-exhaustive discovery.
