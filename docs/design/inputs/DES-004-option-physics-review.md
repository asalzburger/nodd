## Verdict

**INFERENCE:** no topology has a demonstrated intrinsic showstopper. The outer
coil conflicts with the unchanged E1-R2 muon host; that is a genuine integration
conflict requiring amendment, not proof that outer solenoids fail. Missing field,
material or fit evidence must not become an asserted physical impossibility.

**NODD DESIGN CHOICE — proposed research priority, approving humans pending:**
advance MAG-05 and MAG-02 as complementary standalone-muon investigations;
retain MAG-04 as a resource-intensive built-topology comparison with conditional
redesign; use MAG-01/03 as field controls and combined-measurement alternatives;
give MAG-06 a bounded finite-coil plausibility screen. This orders research,
not performance winners.

## Challenges by option

- **MAG-01:** its compact vacuum solution and central field do not establish
  forward tracking or sufficient standalone muon information. Calorimeter steel
  prevents interpreting the vacuum control as its physical field.
- **MAG-02:** modest equivalent return area leaves a credible question to test,
  not a solved yoke. Concentrating flux can increase local field while decreasing
  path length; useful bending must occur between independent measurements and
  survive scattering, energy loss and forward apertures.
- **MAG-03:** reduced tracker end-field variation motivates propagation but does
  not establish momentum improvement. The r=4.50 m diagnostic muon surface lies
  on its current sheet and cannot count as a physical measurement. Compare
  compatible station sets explicitly; do not quietly improve one candidate's layout.
- **MAG-04:** 190.85 Wb divided by 66.67 m² gives 2.86 T only under the stated
  flat-bore/all-flux/solid-annulus assumptions. It is a strong space-pressure
  warning, not a nonlinear saturation calculation or hard rejection. Actual
  bore flux, steel distribution, air return and calorimeter contributions matter.
  Larger radius is one remedy; changed flux, return paths or station allocation
  are alternatives requiring explicit studies.
- **MAG-05:** toroidal orientation gives a credible standalone/forward mechanism,
  but the limited host and discrete coil sectors could defeat it. ATLAS precedent
  does not supply nODD's lever arm, alignment or angular completeness. Priority
  alongside MAG-02 is defensible; demonstrated superiority is not.
- **MAG-06:** the fourth-concept precedent is a proposal, not operating evidence.
  Its quoted angular reach falls short of the nODD requirement. The 7.79 m
  return-radius screen is an optimistic zero-thickness annulus model starting at
  the main sheet; physical coil clearance reduces usable space. Finite ends,
  current coupling and forward measurements remain decisive.

## Requirements to freeze before ranking

Agree momentum/vertex samples; separate unconstrained standalone, vertex-constrained
and combined goals; minimum angular completeness across η/φ; measurement and
alignment assumptions; material/survival accounting; allowable envelope changes;
and resource/stray-field criteria. Distinguish numerical tolerances from physics
acceptance. Until those choices exist, report tradeoffs and unresolved differences
rather than weighted scores or detector acceptance.
