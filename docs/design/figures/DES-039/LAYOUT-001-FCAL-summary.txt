Tungsten was chosen to provide a large absorber depth within LAYOUT-001’s narrow forward envelope.
Quartz fibres provide Cherenkov light and radiation tolerance, drawing on the established CMS forward-calorimeter technology.
Rear fibre extraction uses the available downstream space for compact readout and cooling.
Each annular module is 1.80 m long, has a 580 mm outer diameter and 100 mm central bore, and occupies |z| = 4.533–6.333 m on either side of the interaction point.
The 1.20 m sampling block contains 24,324 fused-silica fibres with 0.6 mm cores on a 3 mm lattice.
Interleaved long and short fibres, with the short population starting 100 mm deeper, provide two overlapping shower-depth weightings.
Sixteen azimuthal sectors, three radial bands and two fibre populations give 96 tower signals per end, read by cooled SiPM arrays behind a 300 mm optical fan-out.
The design remains a prototype, with machine clearance, radiation lifetime, shower response and engineering qualification still pending.
