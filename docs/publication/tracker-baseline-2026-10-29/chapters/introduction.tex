\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}
This report documents the selected nODD silicon tracker as a baseline for
technical review. It develops the pixel, short-strip and long-strip assemblies
from their sensors and local structures through barrel/endcap placement and
common service interfaces. The tracker chapter preserves both successful model
checks and the engineering and coverage cases that remain unresolved.

The report is a living design specification. Public detector experience informs
its technologies and construction concepts; nODD dimensions and modeling
choices are explained separately. The present review edition does not claim
complete detector integration, construction qualification or an accepted
performance release. Other detector chapters will be developed against their
own reviewed subsystem specifications.
