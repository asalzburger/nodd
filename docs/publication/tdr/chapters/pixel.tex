\section{Selected baseline and applicability}
The preliminary baseline combines the unchanged four-layer DES013 barrel with
nine discs at each end. Endcaps use the DES017 mixed single/quad template,
trimmed by the DES018 luminous-region and track-envelope proof. The isolated
DD4hep compact implements this selection under DES019; the old quad-only
compact remains a historical control. Baseline selection does not grant
engineering sign-off \cite{barrel,mixed,trim,model}.

The combined inventory contains \num{\PixelModules} modules and
\num{\PixelChips} active chip areas. The unchanged barrel contributes
\num{3050} modules and \num{6794} active areas; the two endcaps contribute
\num{\EndcapModules} modules and \num{\EndcapChips} active areas
\cite{barrel,model}. These counts describe sensitive islands. Each quad shares
one silicon substrate across four active islands.

The trimming proof applies to primary charged tracks with
$\pt\geq\qty{1}{\giga\electronvolt}$ and $\abseta\leq4$, luminous
$z$ within $\pm\qty{150}{\milli\metre}$, and transverse vertex coordinates
$x,y\in[-1,1]\,\unit{\milli\metre}$. It assumes a uniform axial field
with magnitude at most \qty{4}{\tesla}, vacuum propagation, and the first
outward half-turn before leaving the comparison host. The closed eta boundary
conservatively implements the requested $\abseta<4$ \cite{trim}.
This is a bounded geometric hypothesis, not a measured beam profile or full
physical acceptance claim. Lower-momentum, secondary, scattered and returning
trajectories require further study.

\section{Endcap layout and inner-ring removal}
The full mixed template has four single-module rings followed by two quad-module
rings: six physical rings containing eight radial chip rows. Modules follow local
tangential and radial axes, with alternating support faces and frozen height
levels. Signed transverse compensation is retained at every disc datum
\cite{mixed,model}.

For positive datum $z_d$, luminous half-length $L$ and frozen local height
$\delta z$, define $a=(z_d^2-L^2)/z_d$. Module centres obey
$(x,y)=(x_0,y_0)(1+\delta z/a)$ and sensor plane $z_d+\delta z$.
Negative placements reflect $z$ and retain the same transverse positions.
Filtering never recolours, moves or compacts survivors \cite{mixed,trim,model}.

Whole inner rings are removed only when every active-area corner lies below
the continuous lower reachable-radius bound, including curvature and the vertex
box, with a \qty{0.1}{\milli\metre} retention buffer. A reachable witness
on the next ring prevents its removal. The selected prefix is maximal within
this frozen catalogue, rather than a global layout optimum \cite{trim}.

\section{Modules and readout orientation}
Each endcap active island is \qty{20}{\milli\metre} tangentially by
\qty{19.2}{\milli\metre} radially, with conditional
\qty{0.1}{\milli\metre} guard and \qty{0.2}{\milli\metre} inactive seams.
Shared silicon outlines are $20.2\times19.4$ for singles and
$40.4\times38.8\,\unit{\milli\metre^2}$ for quads. Occupied body reservations
are $20.4\times21.4$ and $43.2\times44.2\,\unit{\milli\metre^2}$,
respectively, with \qty{1}{\milli\metre} thickness \cite{radial,model}.
These are project hypotheses, not procurement specifications.

The reused stack has \qty{0.15}{\milli\metre} sensor and ASIC silicon,
\qty{0.02}{\milli\metre} bump stand-off and inherited flex/glue layers.
Nominal \qty{50}{\micro\metre} square segmentation remains unchanged and
provisional \cite{barrel}. The physical tangential/radial convention supports
future non-square segmentation without rotating measurement interpretation with
global azimuth. Frame signs establish a proper rotation and place the ASIC side
toward its pickup; tangential/radial dimensions are not swapped \cite{model}.

\section{Local support, cooling and mounting}
The common support retains $r=27$--$188.5\,\unit{\milli\metre}$ and the
\qty{6.3}{\milli\metre} sandwich: two \qty{0.15}{\milli\metre} CFRP skins
around a \qty{6}{\milli\metre} foam core. Three inherited tongues couple to
rails and the carrier, whose outer radius remains \qty{232}{\milli\metre}.
Coupling details and installation tolerances are unresolved \cite{mixed,model}.

Every retained chip has a \qty{0.45}{\milli\metre} cradle/graphite/insulator/
TIM/bond pickup. Graphite stems have $6\times8\,\unit{\milli\metre^2}$
cross-section; quad stems move \qty{1}{\milli\metre} toward the module centre.
Raised stems follow frozen \qty{1.65}{\milli\metre} level spacing. Removed
rows delete their pickups and cooling demand. Closing unused skin/core windows
while preserving structural and thermal interfaces remains a manufacturing task
\cite{mixed,trim,model}.

Each retained chip row uses two half-ring circuits, giving
\num{\CoolingCircuits} circuits across both ends. Tube outer diameter is
\qty{2.8}{\milli\metre}, with \qty{0.15}{\milli\metre} titanium wall;
core routing planes lie at $\pm\qty{1.5}{\milli\metre}$. Transport feed/return
diameter stays \qty{4}{\milli\metre} \cite{mixed,model}. Flow estimates inherit
nominal/stress power and boiling-quality hypotheses, not measured hydraulics.

DD4hep uses complete titanium/CO$_2$ tori to preserve summed half-ring length.
Radial legs and thermal inserts use an explicit effective core allocation;
torus daughters displace the core. Skin/cradle window inventories replace CFRP
with graphite once per chip. Bend allowances, clips, mount hardware and retained
flex inventories are normalized into local service cells. Constituent volumes
and masses are conserved without stacking full foam and occupied solids
\cite{model}. This allocation differs from DES017's approximate mass proxy;
it is not detailed CAD, hydraulic connectivity or a manufactured bill of materials.

\section{Services and qualification limits}
Collectors retain $r=27$--$190\,\unit{\milli\metre}$; the trunk remains
$r=192$--$231.7\,\unit{\milli\metre}$ and flange neck ends at
\qty{222}{\milli\metre}. Heterogeneous inventories use each disc's actual
chains, links and chip-row circuits. The first eight discs join the trunk;
the last follows the inherited bypass \cite{trim,model}.

Reference packing utilization is \num{\PositiveTrunkUtil} in the positive
trunk and \num{\PositiveNeckUtil} at its neck. Negative values are
\num{\NegativeTrunkUtil} and \num{\NegativeNeckUtil}. All exceed unity;
conservative/stress scenarios also fail \cite{trim,model}. Effective cells
preserve material demand but do not establish a feasible packed route.
Connectors, manifolds, flange passage, bypass and cable topology require further
engineering. Service radii are not enlarged to conceal these failures.

\section{Implementation evidence and next steps}
Native DD4hep construction checked all \num{\PixelChips} active placements,
signed axes, normals, dimensions, unique identifiers and readouts. Maximum centre
residual is \qty{\CentreResidual}{\milli\metre}. Exclusive material mass/volume
and sampled radiation/interaction-length scans pass. ROOT finds no overlaps
at \qty{1e-5}{\milli\metre}; ROOT and portable nodehammer roundtrips pass.
Geant4 conversion and FTFP\_BERT initialization pass with seed42 and zero
transported events \cite{model}.

Simulation material inventory totals \qty{\ModelMassKg}{\kilogram}, including
effective shared services; this is not a manufactured mass estimate. Native
execution used the recorded dirty implementation tree, identified by actual
compact/input/producer/plugin hashes. Its execution revision is distinct from
the enclosing deliverable commit \cite{model}.

DD4hep encodes template ID offsets without compacting survivors and retains
their original global source IDs in the inventory. The inherited DES018 study
compared \num{1906} analytic and \num{182} in-scope native tracks per variant,
without removing an in-scope baseline hit \cite{trim}. This is not a
DD4hep-to-ACTS conversion test. Material transport, realized field map,
digitisation, reconstruction, alignment and efficiency/resolution studies remain.

Original coverage gaps persist. The \qty{0.5}{\milli\metre} guard and warm
$-35\,\unit{\degreeCelsius}$ thermal controls fail \cite{mixed,trim}.
Thermal contacts, two-phase stability, mechanical tolerances, window closure
and routing are principal engineering gates. The model provides a reproducible
preliminary comparison baseline while these questions remain open.
