\section{Strip mounting interfaces}
\label{sec:strip-mounting-interfaces}
The cooled carbon sandwich carries the silicon, local electronics and services
in both strip systems. In the short-strip system the modules sit on one face
through graphite pickups. In the long-strip system the two sensors of each
stereo pair attach to opposite faces of the same structural sandwich. Raising
a pair increases both outward pickup heights while leaving that common carrier
in place. \Cref{fig:strip-mounting-interfaces} follows these local loads through
the bearing or petal contacts to their external support interfaces.

The mounting schemes describe simulation geometry and engineering hypotheses.
The long-barrel gravity screen includes core shear and a separate joint
allocation; it assumes stiff bearing reactions. The long-petal horizontal
handling case exceeds its displacement screen, requiring a temporary handling
frame and plate/joint/global-ring analysis. Adhesive stresses during cooldown,
torsion, local load introduction, service forces and vibration remain open across
the four assemblies. A successful geometry construction or overlap check does
not qualify these mechanical interfaces.
